Po' Boy Steak Sandwich: A classic New Orleans-style sandwich loaded with savory steak and your favorite toppings.

Ingredients:

  • Ribeye steak
  • Toasted bread
  • Pickles
  • Sliced tomatoes
  • Toasted bread

Steps:

  1. Season the ribeye steak with salt and pepper. Let it sit at room temperature for approximately 15 minutes.
  2. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the steak for about 3-4 minutes per side.
  3. Let the steak rest for about 5 minutes, then cut into thin slices.
  4. Toast the bread in the skillet until lightly golden and crisp.
  5. Layer the sliced steak onto the toasted bread. Add lettuce, sliced tomatoes, and pickles.
  6. Enjoy 😊.
